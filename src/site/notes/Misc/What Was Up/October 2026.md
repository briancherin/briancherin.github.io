- New experience: had my first ever cavity filled and drilled. I was semi-nervous leading up to it, though honestly more nervous *about* being nervous and less so nervous for the actual procedure. Indeed, the hardest part wasn't the anesthetic injection, but my mental reaction to it - I got warm and sweaty in the minutes afterwards, a not too unexpected result as I have had similar moments of health anxiety in the past. Something about my body being affected by something external and mysterious, fear of losing control maybe, or just getting in my head being nervous about how my body is reacting to it. I felt better after venting some air into my shirt, and the dentist gave me a damp paper towel to cool my forehead, and then I felt normal after a minute. The actual drilling+filling itself was fine, the worst was a couple brief moments of slight sensitivity where they were drilling, but not sustained enough for it to be an issue. Glad to have gotten this over with. Vaguely nervous about the future of it because I've heard that once you have one filling, it's easier for that tooth to get decayed again and can lead to increasingly larger issues until it turns into a root canal. Hopefully doesn't happen though.
