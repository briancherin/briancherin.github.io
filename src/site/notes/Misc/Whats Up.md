What's up. What's going on. What am I doing, reading, interesting stuff to share that I don't feel like making a separate page for. I don't expect myself to keep this super regularly updated or fully comprehensive of my activity - but it's a place to put stuff that I find noteworthy.

### August 2026
- TV
	- Rewatching ATLA last month and finishing catching up on Rick & Morty is reminding me how good animated series can be. 
- Creative thoughts
	- Also, as usually happens when I consume good art (see "TV" above), it makes *me* want to *create*... Trying to come to terms with this desire within me to create some kind of animated story. When I've had this feeling in the past (well, maybe more for feeling inspired to create a live action film or shorts or whatever), I come to the logic that the baby steps are to first just [[Interests/Creative/Writing/Creative Writing\|write creatively]], then for animation get better at [[Interests/Creative/Drawing/Drawing People From Imagination\|drawing]], which could lead to creating [[Interests/Creative/Drawing/Creating Webcomics\|storyboards/comics]] , which doesn't feel too far away from animation (except of course realizing that animation is extremely time-expensive, but knowing me, I might put a medium amount of effort interspersed over an annoyingly long period of time and end up creating a mediocre three minute long animation and then be satisfied and call it quits). Brief youtube spree searching for "How to make a cartoon": ["Making an Indie Animated Series from Scratch" (Infinimart)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-CkwJKbWRr4) makes it very scary, whereas ["Animating a Whole Cartoon in 24 Hours" (CyanSandwich)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uZ3kXp1MTA) is more comforting -- makes me more confident that there is an in-between space between these two where I could create something satisfying.
- Blog commentary
	- Adding a couple kind of random (but pre-existing) ideas to the Projects->*3D Modeling* & *Animation and VFX* sections. I want to document more of the random ideas that are floating around, both simply to share them and also to have a place for me to track progress on them (and also creating pages for ideas lets me reference them in other notes when they are relevant of course.)
