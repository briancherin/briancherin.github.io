	- Watched [[Media I Have Consumed/Movies/They Live (1988)\|They Live (1988)]]
	- Catching up on *Daredevil: Born Again* season 2. I started it a couple months ago but wasn't in the right mindspace to continue (maybe just because it's a darker type of show) but I'm enjoying it now that I've actually gotten into it.
- Creative thoughts
	- Comics
		- As usually happens when I consume good art (see "TV" above), it makes *me* want to *create*... Trying to come to terms with this desire within me to create some kind of animated story. When I've had this feeling in the past (well, maybe more for feeling inspired to create a live action film or shorts or whatever), I come to the logic that the baby steps are to first just [[Interests/Creative/Writing/Creative Writing\|write creatively]], then for animation get better at [[Interests/Creative/Drawing/Drawing People From Imagination\|drawing]], which could lead to creating [[Interests/Creative/Drawing/Creating Webcomics\|storyboards/comics]], which doesn't feel too far away from animation (except of course realizing that animation is extremely time-expensive, but knowing me, I might put a medium amount of effort interspersed over an annoyingly long period of time and end up creating a mediocre three minute long animation and then be satisfied and call it quits). Brief youtube spree searching for "How to make a cartoon": ["Making an Indie Animated Series from Scratch" (Infinimart)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-CkwJKbWRr4) makes it very scary, whereas ["Animating a Whole Cartoon in 24 Hours" (CyanSandwich)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-uZ3kXp1MTA) is more comforting -- makes me more confident that there is an in-between space between these two where I could create something satisfying.
		- I've also started reading through "Understanding Comics"  by Scott McCloud ([[Media I Have Consumed/Books/Nonfiction/Understanding Comics by Scott McCloud\|notes]]).
- Programming projects
	- I'm gotten an urge to think through some game dev ideas. Not sure what will come of them. Possibly this is triggered by me working on a game-ish project for my company hackathon. Most of my ideas right now are involving LLM-based interaction or user-directed AI-generated assets in some way. e.g. Thinking about [[Random Topics/AI Person Simulation\|AI Person Simulation]] and [[Random Topics/Interactive Narrative\|Interactive Narrative]].
	- I've occasionally been using Codex (Open AI's CLI-based AI tool) to help with local development. After getting frustrated at reaching the weekly credit limit, I decided to try one month of the pro-tier ($80/month) which highly increases the limit. I've ended up making progress on several projects due to trying to then hit that limit so as to make use of what I've paid for. There a strange balance of finding enough work for it to do, versus then still eventually running out of quota and then having to wait for the limit to reset the next week. I'm not currently sure if I would do another renewal of this or just stick to the regular plus-tier ($20/month) which I've been on basically since ChatGPT came out.
	- Been making progress on [[Projects/Software/Grocery Receipt Tracker\|Grocery Receipt Tracker]]. I'm partially feeling spurred to make progress here because I discovered a competitor who has actually released something that is almost exactly my same idea. I'm feeling mixed between thereby not wanting to continue it, or wanting to continue it and make it better than theirs. I'm at least giving it a try and we'll see how far I go.
	- Started making a prototype for [[Projects/Software/Idea - Social ebook annotation\|Idea - Social ebook annotation]] - I'm quite excited about this idea, and the goal (at first at least) is for a community tool that's more open-source rather than profit/product-driven. I just genuinely feel like this should be a thing that exists and that people should use, and I feel that I don't want to put a barrier (like a paywall) in front of it.

I want to document more of the random ideas that are floating around, both simply to share them and also to have a place for me to track progress on them (and also creating pages for ideas lets me reference them in other notes when they are relevant of course.)
