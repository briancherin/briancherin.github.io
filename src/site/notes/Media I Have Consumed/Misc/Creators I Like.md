##### ReWild University
A lovely channel run by Kenton Whitman. So much wisdom to be found in these videos. He used to run a "Forest Monk" program where people would come to the forest and get away from technology.

##### Robin Greenfield
Similar category of content as ReWildU. He does over-the-top lifestyle experiments as a form of activism (as opposed to trying to get others should mimic him). Examples include wearing his own trash, eliminating all possessions, dumpster diving for food, and living for a year only on foraged food. In his book "Food Freedom" (which talks about [[Lifestyle/Foraging/Foraging\|foraging]] as well) he mentions [[Media I Have Consumed/Books/Nonfiction/Braiding Sweetgrass by Robin Wall Kimmerer\|Braiding Sweetgrass]] as one of his greatest influences.
