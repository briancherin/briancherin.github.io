Some songs instantly bring me back to a different time in my life, often a specific moment when I was listening to them. It could be an overall album or artist, too.

There are more than the below list, but this is just what I'm starting with.

- Album: "A Kiss Before You Go" by Katzenjammer
	- Autumn vibes. Brings me back to walking downhill along the street by where I lived senior year of college. Rustling and crunching leaves.
- Song: "Innocence" by Renaissance
	- Especially the line "And sometimes when the clouds obscure the sun, I wonder my my life feels as narrow as the road that winds along its way" -- brings me back to a certain eclipse viewing at a park on a blanket with friends. This scene is also in my mind as a candidate for a [[Projects/3D and VR/Personal 3D VR World Building\|Personal 3D VR World Building]] scene.
