Published 1993. Structured as a sort of comic strip going through the history and mechanics (?) of comics. Reading due to my interest in [[Interests/Creative/Drawing/Creating Webcomics\|Creating Webcomics]].

Notes
- Constructs a definition of comics: "Juxtaposed pictorial and other images in deliberate sequence, intended to convey information and/or to produce an aesthetic response in the viewer."
- Walked through historical examples (even from thousands of years ago) of "comics" that fit into that definition.
