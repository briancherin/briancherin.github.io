Notes from listening to this episode:
- Dont spend too much time on one startup idea 
- Want to make "indie music" - not something that will universally appeal to everyone, but something he likes. Follow gut to make decisions on what to build rather than just following metrics. (Still test/observe features obviously)
- Make stuff that solves problems for you - you're the expert of your own problem. 
- Having a unique lifestyle or doing unique things in your life gives you an advantage of finding problems to solve which other people haven't already solved 
- Don't be arrogant that your thing is the best and is going to work. Validate and see if it's true. Most things won't work out, like dating. "Maybe we're too used to things working out". You have to be aware that there's a chance it won't work out (good dating advice too)
- If your goal is to make money in a certain amount of time, obviously focus on making monetizable apps. (Point is it's ok to make free stuff too but if you're like running out of rent then obviously you should try to make money)
- Strategy of taking an existing thing and applying it to a new idea (eg indiehackers forum kind of copied based on nomad list forum, in forming a directory of people with a shared interest)
- Look at existing products, ask how i can make them better
- Interesting that they're talking about forums when one of my current ideas/projects is making a [[Projects/Software/Book Forum\|book forum]].
- Talking about his usage of Twitter to express his thoughts, ideas, life stuff. "I'm constantly thinking about stuff and then putting it on Twitter. Twitter's like my brain, it's very public." Transparency, not hiding secrets -- concept of getting ahead of the curve of data leaks and stuff (I think this is a big tongue in cheek). Don't have to worry about leaks if you are transparent anyway. Says though there is a narcissistic element of enjoying the attention, his goal is not to have a following, but instead he's mainly just dumping his brain on the internet. 
	- (Connects to my habit of this digital garden, and also makes me wonder if I should engage a little bit on Twitter (X) or at least share out blog posts there. I have a negative connotation of X so I'm not sure I ever would.)
