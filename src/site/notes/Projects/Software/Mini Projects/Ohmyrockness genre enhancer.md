[Oh My Rockness](https://www.ohmyrockness.com/) is a website that lists music shows in NYC. It also lets you filter by shows that are free to attend. I've never actually attended a show through the site, but I've wanted to. My one qualm with it is that it does not easily show the genre of the bands or a description of the kind of music they play. There are so many event listings that it's hard to make a choice of which I might be interested in.

For a while, I've had an idea floating in my mind to create some way to easily tell what the genres of the bands are.

Today I decided to have an AI coding session to come up with a solution. And it worked out pretty well!
