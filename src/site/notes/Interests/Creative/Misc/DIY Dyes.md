>[!INFO]- AI Chat
>"How would i make my own dyes out of natural materials i can find in nyc? For art/paint/something? I'm complete noob"
>
>> ### Natural dyes you can make in NYC (beginner-friendly)
> 
> **Scope:** simple pigment extraction for paper, ink washes, and sketchbook art. No textile chemistry required.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 1) What “natural dye” means here
> 
> Extract color compounds from plants/foods using water (sometimes vinegar or baking soda). Expect experimentation and some fading over time.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 2) Easy starter materials (NYC-available)
> 
> **Kitchen scraps**
> 
> - Onion skins (yellow/red) → gold, rust, orange-brown
>     
> - Avocado pits/skins → dusty pink, peach
>     
> - Red cabbage → purple → blue/green with baking soda
>     
> - Coffee or black tea → browns/sepias
>     
> - Turmeric → intense yellow (not lightfast)
>     
> 
> **Foraged (parks/markets)**
> 
> - Leaves → yellow-green
>     
> - Berries (even inedible ones) → purples/blues (often fade)
>     
> - Walnut shells (farmers markets) → deep brown
>     
> - Dandelion roots → warm yellow-brown
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 3) Noob-proof extraction
> 
> **You need**
> 
> - Small pot or jar
>     
> - Water
>     
> - Strainer/cloth
>     
> - Watercolor or sketch paper
>     
> - Old brush
>     
> 
> **Steps**
> 
> 1. Chop/crush plant material.
>     
> 2. Cover with water (2–3× volume).
>     
> 3. Simmer 20–60 min (or steep overnight).
>     
> 4. Strain.
>     
> 5. Paint directly with the liquid.
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 4) Make it act more like paint or ink
> 
> **Thicken**
> 
> - A few drops of gum arabic (art store).
>     
> - Optional: tiny drop of honey for flow.
>     
> 
> **Shift color**
> 
> - Vinegar → more red/pink.
>     
> - Baking soda → blue/green shift (especially cabbage).
>     
> 
> Test on scrap paper.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 5) Improve longevity (basic)
> 
> - Add 1–2 tsp vinegar per cup of dye (helps on paper).
>     
> - Store in the fridge.
>     
> - Fading over weeks/months is normal.
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 6) Best surfaces
> 
> - Watercolor paper (best)
>     
> - Sketch paper or handmade paper
>     
> - Avoid glossy/coated paper
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 7) Safe NYC foraging rules
> 
> - Avoid dog-heavy areas and roadsides.
>     
> - Wash everything.
>     
> - Never taste pigments.
>     
> - If unsure about a plant, skip it.
>     
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 8) First mini-project
> 
> **Kitchen dye sampler:** onion skins, red cabbage, coffee, turmeric.  
> Make four jars, label them, paint swatches, then modify each with vinegar/baking soda.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 9) Next steps (optional)
> 
> - Lake pigments (turn dye into dry pigment)
>     
> - Iron modifiers (blackening inks)
>     
> - Historical inks (walnut ink, oak gall)
>     
>
