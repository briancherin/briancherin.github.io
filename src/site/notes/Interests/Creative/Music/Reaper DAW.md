I've been using Reaper, which is a DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) for music production. It's free and lightweight. A lot of people seem to like it and apparently it can do things just as well as the fancier-seeming DAWs. I think a lot of its potential is unlocked through plugins.

There's a [set of videos](https://www.reaper.fm/videos.php) on the Reaper website that is supposed to be good for learning some basics. I haven't really gone through it but I've watched a couple. I have a lot to learn.

Found a tutorial series that seems a good way to learn some fundamentals: "How to create a pop song using Reaper" on youtube channel *Noteoriously Naomi (Naomi Ryde)*: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CsaOE9dnmJY. So far I'm only midway through the first video.
