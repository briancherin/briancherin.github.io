I like to highlight passes that stand out to me in e-books^[I read 99% of books in e-book form]. Over the years I've developed a sort of loose color code for when I highlight something:

- yellow for something in the writing that just generally stands out
- blue for something that is funny according to some inside joke with myself or an allusion to other material
- red for the sage life advice stuff that I want to mark as something significant for me to remember and look back on
- green for something related to my specific reason for reading the book, or more lately, just a catch-all if I want to add commentary on my experience of reading the book

When I [[Projects/Software/Play Books Notes Quiz and Browser\|look back at my highlights]], I then have a sense of why I highlighted each one, based on its color.
