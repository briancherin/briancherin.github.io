I'm generally a pretty happy person. But there are some days when I am tired or drained - physically, socially, or emotionally. And I lose the rhythm. My mind can become clouded. Doing the dishes can feel overwhelming. I am not sure what to do but sit on the couch. 

A voice inside me calls, and says, "Listen to music."

And I do. And then something stirs, something changes. The void in my mind is infiltrated by notes, melodies, words, imagery, rhymes, *drums*. Those drums are my heartbeat. The rhythm makes my head bob, barely a conscious act. And suddenly, this derailed train is back on track and rumbling forward, and everything is easier. Even physical weariness seems to fade when my mind is flooded with the cacophony of familiar tunes. There is a bounce, in tempo with the music, to my steps.

The voice inside me is important. I can forget the momentum that music can bring. I think, also, that this is part of why I like the energy and forcefulness of rock music.
