Dreams and dreaming will never stop being a subject of unrelenting fascination to me.

It's not so much an intellectual interest (though there is some of that) as a quest for tangible experience. I want to breathe, feel, taste in my dreams. I want endless adventure and exploration and discovery by means of my dreams. I want to go to sleep at night eager for what awaits me in my mind. I want the sensations, experiences, and memories of my dreams to inspire creative outlets and personal growth in my waking life.
