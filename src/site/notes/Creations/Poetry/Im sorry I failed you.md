I'm sorry,
I failed you,
I was not ready to be a Parent.
It is my fault,
You were left to dry
And I wasn't paying attention
until it was too late.
I see your tear and your drops of blood.
I will think of you for two days
And then you will be forgotten
It is harsh
It is all that is wrong
But I have to move on.
Your soul will continue rotting in its place.
Maybe beautiful.
