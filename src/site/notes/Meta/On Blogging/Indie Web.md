#### Notes on "The Web Revival Breakdown"
https://myrrh.city/museum/library/Thewebrevivalbreakdown
- "The web revival rejects algorithmic content, in favor of intentional and thoughtful expression."
- "There is no scarcity here but your own creative thoughts. Save my images, copy pieces of my code, take inspiration from my site.... Copy my site page for page, but only I’ve got the mind to make new pages just as good as the old.
- "Leave your echo chambers!... A lot of conversations you think may be obvious just haven't happened in other places, and vice versa."
- "Decentralize your web use" -- new phrasing for me for having a personal site. I like it.
- Free online photoshop clone: https://www.photopea.com/
- "[The web revival is] your chance to participate and express yourself and your ideas, and see other peoples as well."
- "Your information is not packed away anywhere for a third party to sell. It is yours. Nearly impossible to be found by anything other than a human who cares."
	- Not sure about the last sentence -- surely a bot could scrape a site like this. Maybe it just means it's not discovery-by-algorithm like most social media.
