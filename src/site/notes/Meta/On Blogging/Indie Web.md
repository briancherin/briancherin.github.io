#### Notes on "The Web Revival Breakdown"
https://myrrh.city/museum/library/Thewebrevivalbreakdown
Reading on: 3/29/2026
- "The web revival rejects algorithmic content, in favor of intentional and thoughtful expression."
- "There is no scarcity here but your own creative thoughts. Save my images, copy pieces of my code, take inspiration from my site.... Copy my site page for page, but only I’ve got the mind to make new pages just as good as the old.
- "Leave your echo chambers!... A lot of conversations you think may be obvious just haven't happened in other places, and vice versa."
- "Decentralize your web use" -- new phrasing for me for having a personal site. I like it.
- Free online photoshop clone: https://www.photopea.com/
- "[The web revival is] your chance to participate and express yourself and your ideas, and see other peoples as well."
- "Your information is not packed away anywhere for a third party to sell. It is yours. Nearly impossible to be found by anything other than a human who cares."
	- Not sure about the last sentence -- surely a bot could scrape a site like this. Maybe it just means it's not discovery-by-algorithm like most social media.
- Talks about part of web revival being about archiving. 
	- Example: Apparently MySpace used to be a place for artists to post their music. It was all deleted from MySpace but there's an archive of it called the "MySpace Dragon Hoard" - this is a player for it: https://mydora.restorativland.org/ 
		- (<-- from that site: "The archived collection contains 490,273 songs, which represents a tiny fraction of tens of millions of songs that were destroyed, many of which had no copies and are lost forever.". How tragic (the destruction).)
	- First thing playing was "Aethra - Far Beyond The Distant Skies", listened to it while writing this. Feels like a radio. Just letting it play as I continue to read the article and write these notes. Another I'm liking is "Mike Joseph - Screeaaam". I like how these have kind of an early digital music feel (meaning audio quality. but maybe it's just my laptop speaker quality).
- "Decentralization of communication means little to no profit incentive. No one paid to sell you on a trend."
- Talks about joining a "web ring", where you join a ring and link to the next site in the chain. Not sure if I would want to do something like this - but it seems like a cool way to have random people find your site and to help visitors to your site find other sites. (This could be an answer to my point in [[Meta/Garden Improvements\|Garden Improvements]] about an alternative to adding this blog to Kagi Small Web since Kagi requires a blog to be 3 years old)
- Just as a touchpoint on my thoughts as of March 2026: I am really appreciating these ideas especially with the ownership aspect in mind. I have felt urges in the past to post deeper stuff to social media, but it never quite felt right. I think one reason is because of the lack of true ownership. With a site like this, I am in charge of my data. This partly is a worry about losing my data. But also it's a feeling of not wanting some corporation to be the place where my data lives -- it's kind of weird. Like, (and yes weird example I'm summoning), imagine someone is building a time capsule (like physical one) and the place they choose (/ feel the need) to hide it in is a fast food restaurant storage closet. Why let your treasured possessions be controlled by an external entity, when you could find an independent location that you are completely in charge of?
