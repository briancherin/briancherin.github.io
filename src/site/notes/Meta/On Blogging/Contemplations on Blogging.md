November 16, 2026

As I write on this site, blogging is something that I have been, and will continue to be, having to contemplate and comprehend. 

Writing blogs, articles, any form of informative prose writing -- feels to me like a natural way of communicating thoughts and opinions. 

Yet not many people do it.

I suppose in many areas, creators are the minority and consumers or lurkers are the majority. I don't mean this to come off in an egotistical way. In fact, I would rather it not be true. It crosses my mind occasionally that I wish more people expressed themselves publicly. But I understand this isn't a necessary thing. People express themselves in their own ways. Most people probably have no desire to have their thoughts publicized. I'm sure that many people have text chains with their friends which match or exceed any quantity of content which I may put up on this site. 

For one reason or another, I do not have such text chains, or at least not to a sufficient degree to satisfy my need to express myself. I think a [[Meta/On Blogging/Reasons to Write (Here)\|reason]] why I have ended up having this website as a place for me to express myself is because I have not had the person or persons with whom I can talk to about the wide range of topics I have in my mind to talk about. Sure, most all topics which I have talked about here are also topics I discuss with friends in real life, but those conversations are spread thin, and generally consist of much less detail than what I am able to put here (at the risk of acknowledging an occasional verbosity in my writing; but the power of this passive medium is that a reader can choose what to read and what not to). 

I ramble a bit, but what I'm trying to say is that writing on a website lets me broadcast my thoughts further and reach many more people, and my hope is for it to kindle connection with people who I may otherwise never have breached certain subjects with. This broadcast-to-connect pipeline is an ideal which I have not exactly reached.

Part of me wishes for more reader engagement; but, and this is getting back to my point about contemplating blogging, it is a medium that I am still learning about and I think it is a medium that people may not be used to engaging with. There is of course the possibility that I am writing about things that simply are of no interest to whatever reader I have in mind. That's fine. But I also wonder if it's an attention span thing and people don't want to read longer-form writing. I'd be less happy with that. Also, most people probably read blogs as things to read, not things to respond to. It doesn't help that I don't have a [[Meta/On Blogging/On Lack of a Comments System\|comments system]]. 

I think there is also some weirdness about this medium being virtual; a friend is likely to engage with me if I am talking about something with them face to face; but for them to read something I wrote, I anticipate there may be some awkwardness in breaching that subject to me then in real life. I wish it weren't so, and it's something to navigate.

Of course, there is also the matter of distribution. Currently anyone reading my site is most likely one of my friends, either one who has subscribed to the newsletter, or someone who has clicked a link in an Instagram story where I occasionally share my notes on. I have listed my site on Kagi Small Web but no other blog index as of now. My traffic from Google Search has also been quite minimal, due to some site indexing issues which I'm working on resolving. All that to say, there are less Internet strangers reading my site than I kind of hope there eventually will be.

All that being said, although it is often quiet around here, it generally does not stop me from continuing to write. So much of the benefit I get from this site is inherent to the simple act of writing and formulating my thoughts, and *having* a place to write (which spurs me to think, formulate, and write).
